HackNHeal // visual systems

Four animated visuals for the website, projection screens and social clips. Every visual is a standalone page you can open full-screen, record, or embed with an iframe.

URL params

?bg=transparenttransparent canvas (compositing, OBS, overlays)
?hud=0clean render, no overlay text
?speed=0.5global time scale
?quality=low|highGPU budget (low = integrated GPUs)
?t=12start the timeline at 12 s
?motion=full|reducedoverride prefers-reduced-motion

Hotkeys

Ssave PNG at 2× resolution
Htoggle HUD
Spacepause / resume
?help overlay (lists visual-specific keys)
